Welcome to the BLOW THINGS UP LAB

WTF is BTU?

The ATLAS BTU Lab is an inclusive community makerspace facilitating projects at the intersection of Art, Design, Engineering and Architecture. It serves multiple functions as a general workspace, and workshop host. Any CU student can gain access to BTU. Our goal is to provide the most diverse, inclusive environment for makers of all types in a highly experimental and productive space.

What can I do in BTU?

The BTU Lab is a space for students to explore and experiment with a variety of materials and techniques. Students can work on personal projects, collaborate with others, or participate in workshops and events. The lab is equipped with tools and resources for woodworking, metalworking, electronics, and more.

How do I get access to BTU?

New members attend a 20 minute general orientation covering community expectations, safety and equipment.
